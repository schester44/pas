# Vendor research: addresses, inspections, IVANS

_Researched 2026-11-18. Supports PLAN.md §15.9 (Addresses) and the TODO.md "Integrations backlog". Every claim links to a source. Prices are list prices from public pages on that date. Anything we could not confirm is marked **(unconfirmed)** and repeated in the [Couldn't confirm](#couldnt-confirm) list._

## Executive summary

- **Addresses: use Smarty as the system of record behind the `AddressProvider` port.** It is the only provider we looked at that combines all of these: CASS/DPV verification, county FIPS, optional rooftop geocodes, a free **stable ID (SmartyKey)**, and terms that allow storing output in our database ([Subscription Agreement §2.5](https://www.smarty.com/legal/terms-of-service)). Two caveats need negotiating in the contract:
  1. Rooftop geocodes must be deleted when the subscription ends unless we buy a "Perpetual Retention Right" (§6.6).
  2. The terms forbid compiling address databases from Smarty output, except for addresses we already hold that are used only to validate, standardize, dedupe, enrich or geocode (§7.3). A per-tenant address registry built from customer-entered addresses looks like it fits that exception, but this needs legal and sales confirmation.
- **Google, Radar and Mapbox (temporary tier) cannot back a permanent address registry.**
  - Google: only the place ID may be stored indefinitely. Standardized address fields and lat/lng are limited to 30 days ([policies](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/address-validation/policies)), and place IDs can change ([place-id](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/place-id)).
  - Radar: storage is capped at 30 days, and using Radar to "develop a database of addresses" is prohibited ([terms §2(d)](https://radar.com/terms)).
  - Mapbox: temporary geocoding results may not be stored ([Mapbox help](https://docs.mapbox.com/help/dive-deeper/understand-temporary-vs-permanent-geocoding/)).
  - All three still work as **suggestion-only** autocomplete if we never persist their output.
- **Cost per bound policy is negligible with any provider.** Assumptions: 10 autocomplete sessions and 1 verification per quote, 25% quote-to-bind (so 40 sessions and 4 verifications per bound policy), about 8 billed keystroke requests per session.
  - Smarty end-to-end: about **$0.60 to $0.90** per bound policy (including rooftop).
  - Cheapest combo (Geoapify or Radar autocomplete, then Smarty verification on select): about **$0.10 to $0.25**.
  - Google: about **$1.15**. Loqate: about **$2.00**.
  - Recommendation: start with a **single provider (Smarty)** for simplicity. The port should allow the autocomplete leg to be swapped for a cheaper provider later, since the verification leg is what feeds the registry.
- **Inspections: no traditional field vendor publishes an API.** CrimsonBlu (the brand of CIS Group; "Crimson Blue" in TODO.md) and Mueller both sell through portals and custom or aggregator integrations under contract.
  - Recommended starting set:
    - **Physical:** CrimsonBlu, which also includes **Capture Q** insured self-surveys with field referral, so one contract covers both physical and self.
    - **Virtual/self:** Flyreel (LexisNexis) or Yembo as the alternative to CrimsonBlu's self-survey.
    - **Imagery:** Nearmap (Betterview) or Moody's (CAPE) for synchronous triage at quote.
  - EagleView has the most developer-friendly portal (self-serve keys, sandbox, webhooks), which makes it useful for building the adapter's contract tests.
- **IVANS:** the MGA/carrier delivers policy, transaction, eDocs, claims and commission files to agency **Y-account mailboxes** on the IVANS network.
  - The dominant download format is ACORD **AL3**. IVANS also accepts ACORD XML and provides translation services to ACORD formats.
  - Expect a carrier/MGA certification project (one PAS vendor reports **6 to 9 months**).
  - Carrier-side pricing is not published (contact sales).
  - The PAS must emit a complete, per-transaction ACORD data record per policy, agency code and line, plus an AL3/XML mapping layer.

### 1.4 Recommendation for the PAS

1. **Smarty as the default `AddressProvider`**, covering both autocomplete and verification.
   - Store `smarty_key` plus the full standardized components, DPV codes, `county_fips`, the geocode and its precision in the per-tenant address registry, keyed by `(provider, provider_address_id)`.
   - Because SmartyKey is unique per _delivery point_, the registry key should also include the secondary designator (unit) when one is present. A building with multiple units should be one parent "location" with child units.
2. **Verify on select, every time.** Run autocomplete in the browser, then call US Street on the server when a suggestion is selected or a free-text address is submitted. Only the server-side verified result goes into the registry. This makes the autocomplete leg swappable: Geoapify or Radar can be added later as a cost-only optimization, with no data-rights impact as long as their output is never persisted.
3. **Contract asks for Smarty:**
   - A **Perpetual Retention Right** for rooftop geocodes. Without it, rooftop coordinates must be deleted within 40 days of termination (§6.6). That is bad for in-force policies, CAT exposure and clearance history.
   - Written confirmation that a multi-tenant SaaS registry of customer-entered addresses is allowed under §7.3, and whether each tenant needs its own licence for data residency.
   - Whether SaaS redistribution to tenants (we resell the lookups) needs a reseller or Custom plan.
4. **Rooftop vs ZIP9 geocodes:** buy rooftop. Surplus-lines municipal/county premium taxes and CAT/wildfire scoring need parcel-level coordinates (PLAN §17.2.5).
5. **Fallback adapter:** Melissa, whose MAK is a persistent key with similar semantics, is the most plausible like-for-like second source if Smarty terms fail. Google is acceptable only as a suggestion source.

**Industry benchmarks** (secondary sources, treat as rough):

- Field inspections typically take 2 to 5 business days from order to report, and longer in Florida hurricane season. Standalone 4-point inspections cost about $75 to $150 at retail ([inspectandtest](https://inspectandtest.net/guides/insurance-home-inspection/), [4-point cost](https://inspectandtest.net/guides/4-point-inspection-cost/)).

**Regulatory caution on imagery:** Texas homeowners have filed complaints over aerial-image-based decisions. State guidance treats imagery as one input, not the sole basis for nonrenewal or declination ([Carrier Management 2026](https://www.carriermanagement.com/features/2026/06/09/293350.htm)). The PAS should require human review, or a confirming inspection, before imagery alone drives an adverse action (ties into the FCRA/adverse-action notices in PLAN §17.3).

### 2.3 Mapping to an "Inspections" integration category

```
InspectionProvider port
  capabilities: [field_exterior, field_interior, four_point, wind_mit, high_value, self_guided, virtual_live, imagery_report]
  order(locationRef→addressRegistryId, type, scope, contacts, dueBy, referenceIds) → providerOrderId
  cancel(providerOrderId) / reschedule
  getStatus(providerOrderId)        // polling fallback
  onEvent(webhook | poll | sftp)    // normalised to InspectionEvent
  getReport(providerOrderId) → { pdf, photos[], findings[], measuredAttributes{} }
  lookup(addressRegistryId) → ImageryResult   // synchronous, imagery vendors only
```

- **Normalized status:**
  - `requested → accepted → (link_sent → insured_started | scheduled) → in_progress → completed → report_available`
  - Exit states: `cancelled`, `unable_to_complete` (no access, insured unresponsive), `referred_to_field` (self → physical, which spawns a child order).
  - The vendor's raw status is always stored alongside.
- **Findings → actions** (via a `inspection_findings` decision in product content, not code):
  - **Hazard with remediation** (trampoline, missing handrail, roof wear): create a **post-bind subjectivity** with a due date and proof required (photo or invoice), using the same obligation and timer machinery as PLAN §17.1. If it's overdue, raise a UW task and then a notice-of-cancellation workflow where allowed.
  - **Attribute mismatch** (roof age/material, square footage, pool): propose an **endorsement or re-rate** and raise a UW review task. Never apply it silently.
  - **Ineligible risk:** raise a UW task with a cancel/non-renew recommendation, then generate notices per `notice_rules`. Imagery-only findings require a confirming inspection or human review.
- **When to order** (decision: `inspection_requirements`): at quote, run the imagery `lookup` and score it. The score routes to "no inspection", "self-inspection" or "field", or to a bind requirement (e.g. high-value homes need an interior inspection pre-bind). Renewals use change alerts from the imagery vendor.
- **Billing:** inspection fees are per-tenant cost lines, optionally passed through as a policy/inspection fee. Taxability is handled in `taxes_fees`.

## 3. IVANS (quick)

**Ownership:** IVANS has been part of Applied Systems since 2013 ([Business Wire](https://businesswire.com/news/home/20130501005947/en/Applied-Systems-Closes-Acquisition-of-IVANS-Property-Casualty-Data-Exchange-Business)).

**What it offers MGAs and carriers:**

- **IVANS Download** sends to agency partners: policy data for commercial lines (Auto, BOP, Property, Umbrella, WC) and personal lines (Auto, Homeowners, Flood, Watercraft), **eDocs & Messages** (e.g. dec pages), **Direct Bill Commission Statements**, and **claims** ([IVANS for MGAs](https://www.ivans.com/for-mgas/products/ivans-download/)).
- **Delivery:** files go to the agency's IVANS **mailbox**, identified by a **Y-Account** (e.g. `YAB1D`). Carriers send once a day or continuously ([Details on Ivans Download PDF](https://int.ivans.com/globalassets/all-documents/resources/brochures-data-sheets/ivans/details-on-ivans-download.pdf)).
- **Agency activation:** the agency gives the carrier/MGA its Y-Account, mailbox and IBM address, plus its agency contract codes.
- **IVANS Exchange:** a free portal for agencies to see connections and request downloads (same PDF; [IVANS Exchange](https://www.ivans.com/for-agents/products/ivans-exchange/)). It also gives carriers and MGAs one place to manage agency download services and connection/usage reports ([InsuranceNewsNet](https://insurancenewsnet.com/oarticle/ivans-launches-ivans-exchange-2016-to-drive-download-adoption)).
- **Translation services:** IVANS will translate policy data into ACORD formats for download, with expertise in AL3 and XML ([professional services](https://ivans.com/about-us/professional-services)). This is a buy-vs-build option for the AL3 mapping.

**Formats:**

- ACORD **AL3** is still the dominant batch download format. Duck Creek says "almost all downloads… are distributed… in AL3" ([Coverager](https://coverager.com/?p=7173)).
- IVANS also supports **ACORD XML** for real-time exchange ([IVANS video](https://int.ivans.com/resources/videos/ivans-supporting-acordr-data-for-agents/)).
- BriteCore, a PAS vendor, sends **XML files overnight** to IVANS for qualifying transactions ([BriteCore](https://help.britecore.com/hc/en-us/articles/8723624886803-BriteSuite-New-IVANS-integration-for-Personal-Auto)).
- AL3 use requires an **ACORD AL3 licence or membership** ([ACORD P&C standards](https://acord.org/standards-architecture/acord-data-standards/Property_Casualty_Data_Standards), [carrier membership](https://www.acord.org/membership-participation/programs-offerings/p-c-carrier-standards-forms-membership)).

**Onboarding and certification:**

- The carrier/MGA registers with IVANS for certification. IVANS exports test policies and helps configure AL3 codes. Certification "usually takes about **6–9 months**" in one PAS vendor's experience ([BriteCore IVANS setup](https://help.britecore.com/hc/en-us/articles/8724261727763-IVANS-setup), [BriteCore](https://help.britecore.com/hc/en-us/articles/8723472807443)).
- IVANS certifies agency management systems during each download implementation, with 41 AMSs certified at the time of that announcement ([InsuranceNewsNet](https://insurancenewsnet.com/oarticle/Insurance-Industry-Continues-to-Take-Major-Steps-Toward-Improving-Agent-Carrier-a-334562)).
- On the agency side, AMS360 needs a Download licence and picks up nightly ([Vertafore help](https://help.vertafore.com/AMS360/content/contextsensitive/download-integration/dlivans_download.htm)).
- AMSs match on insured and policy: add, update insured, or change ([Momentum AMP](https://support.momentumamp.com/support/solutions/articles/156000373369-carrier-downloads-ivans-setup-process-and-expectations)). Stable policy numbers and insured identity across transactions are therefore essential.

**Cost model:**

- IVANS Exchange is free to agencies (except a Citizens of Florida fee) ([IVANS Exchange](https://www.ivans.com/for-agents/products/ivans-exchange/)).
- **Carrier/MGA fees are not published** (contact sales).
- **Alternative channel:** Vertafore runs its own Carrier/MGA Download product built on ACORD standards ([Vertafore](https://www.vertafore.com/products/insurance-policy-details/carrier-mga-download)).

**What the PAS must produce:**

1. A **download event per issued transaction** (NB, endorsement, renewal, cancel, reinstate, audit, rewrite). PLAN §17.1 already has an `ams_download` obligation kind. Use it so delivery has status, retries and proof (IVANS acknowledgement).
2. A **canonical ACORD-shaped transaction record**: insured, producer/agency code per carrier (the AMS matching key), policy number and term, LOB, coverages/limits/deductibles per location, premium by coverage, taxes and fees (SL tax and stamping fee lines), additional interests (mortgagees), and transaction effective date and change detail.
3. A **mapping layer** to AL3 group/record codes per LOB, plus ACORD XML. Build it ourselves under an ACORD licence, or buy IVANS translation.
4. **Agency registry fields:** IVANS Y-Account, mailbox, agency contract code per carrier/MGA relationship, and which downloads each agency wants.
5. **eDocs:** a PDF of dec pages, endorsements and notices attached to the transaction. **DBCS:** commission statements generated from Billing for direct-bill business.
6. A **resend/replay** capability (agencies request resends through IVANS Exchange) and a certification test harness that builds fixture policies for each LOB and transaction type.
